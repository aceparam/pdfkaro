## How to sign a PDF

1. Choose the PDF file. You can also drop it anywhere on this page, or paste it.
2. Type your name, draw your signature with a finger, a mouse or a pen, or choose a picture of it.
3. Choose which pages to sign and where on the page.
4. Press “Sign and download”. When the new file is ready, press Download.

## Questions

### Is my file uploaded?

No. The signature is added in your browser, on your device. The file, your name, your drawing and your picture are not uploaded or sent anywhere. The page keeps them only while this tab is open, and lets go of them when you press Start again or close or leave the tab.

### Where can the signature go?

On the first, the last or every page, at one of six places along the top or bottom edge. It cannot yet be placed on a signature line in the middle of a page. It keeps its own shape and shows over pictures. A form field or note in the same place can cover it.

### Is this a digital signature?

No. Your name, drawing or picture is drawn on the page, as if you had signed a printed copy and scanned it. It does not prove who signed, and anyone with a PDF editor can copy or remove it. This page does not make a digital signature with a certificate.

### Which characters can I type?

Letters from English and other Western European languages, such as é, ü and ñ, numbers and common marks, up to 50 characters, drawn in a plain italic typeface. Names in Hindi, Telugu, Arabic, Chinese and other scripts cannot be typed for now, nor some letters such as Ł. Draw your signature or use a picture of it instead.

### What picture works best?

A JPG or PNG of your signature alone. A PNG with a see-through background lets the page show around it. A photo of a signature on paper brings the paper with it, as a pale box, so crop it first. A very large picture makes the new file large too.

### What stays the same?

Text, links, form fields and what was typed in them, notes, bookmarks, attached files, the document's details and accessibility tags. Pages you did not choose are left as they were. The signature is not added to the tags, so a screen reader may not mention it.

### What does not stay the same?

The new file is written afresh, so earlier saved versions stored inside the original are not kept, and a digital signature already in the file will no longer be valid. A file made to meet PDF/A, for long-term keeping, no longer does.

### Can I use a PDF that has a password?

Yes, if you know the password. The page asks for it, and the password stays in your browser. The new file has no password and no restrictions.

### Is my original file changed?

No. Signing report.pdf gives a new file, report-signed.pdf.
