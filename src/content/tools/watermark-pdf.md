## How to add a watermark

1. Choose the PDF file. You can also drop it anywhere on this page, or paste it.
2. Under “Watermark text”, type the words you want on every page, such as Draft or Copy for review.
3. Press “Add watermark and download”, or press Enter in the box.
4. When the new file is ready, press Download.

## Questions

### Is my file uploaded?

No. The watermark is added in your browser, on your device. The file and the text you type are not uploaded or sent anywhere. Once you choose a file, the page loads only the parts of this site it needs, and they carry nothing from your file or your text. The page keeps the file and the text only while this tab is open, and lets go of them when you press Start again or close or leave the tab.

### What does the watermark look like?

Your text, once, across the middle of each page, rising from the lower left to the upper right. It is grey and partly see-through, so the page under it stays readable, and it shows over pictures and scans. A short word is large and a longer line smaller, so it fits each page with a margin all round, even on a page that is turned or a different size.

### Which characters can I use?

Letters from English and other Western European languages, such as é, ü, ñ and ø, numbers and common marks, up to 50 characters on one line. Text in Hindi, Telugu, Arabic, Chinese and other scripts cannot be used for now, and neither can some letters from other alphabets, such as Ł. The page names any it cannot use.

### Can someone remove the watermark?

Yes. A watermark marks a file but does not protect it. Anyone with a PDF editor can remove it or cover it. Some PDF readers also include its words when the text of a page is copied or searched.

### What stays the same?

Text, links, form fields and what was typed in them, notes, bookmarks, attached files, the document's details and accessibility tags. The watermark is marked as a watermark, so a screen reader can skip it. Form fields and notes are drawn above the page, so one in the middle of a page can cover part of the watermark.

### What does not stay the same?

The new file is written afresh, so earlier saved versions stored inside the original are not kept, and a digital signature will no longer be valid. A file made to meet PDF/A, for long-term keeping, no longer does.

### Can I use a PDF that has a password?

Yes, if you know the password. The page asks for it, and the password stays in your browser. The new file has no password and no restrictions.

### Is my original file changed?

No. You get a new file and your original stays as it was. Adding a watermark to report.pdf gives report-watermarked.pdf.
