## How to turn pictures into one PDF

1. Choose your pictures. JPG and PNG files can be mixed: photos, scans and screenshots together. You can also drop them anywhere on this page, or paste them.
2. Put them in the order you want with “Move up” and “Move down”, or drag them. The first picture is page 1.
3. Press “Convert and download”.
4. When the PDF is ready, press Download. It is named after the first picture: IMG_2041.jpg gives IMG_2041.pdf.

## Questions

### Are my pictures uploaded?

No. The PDF is made in your browser, on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere. When you press Start again or leave the page, the page forgets the pictures and the PDF.

### Can I mix JPG and PNG pictures in one PDF?

Yes. Each picture becomes one page, whatever its kind, in the order shown in the list. A JPG is kept exactly as it was compressed, and a PNG is kept without loss, so neither is made worse.

### How are the pictures fitted to the pages?

Every page is A4. A picture taller than wide gets an upright page; a wider one gets a page on its side, so a mix of photos and screenshots can give pages turned both ways. Each picture is centred and made as large as fits, with no margin, but never larger than its own size, so a small picture is not blurred. Photos from a phone are turned the right way up.

### Can I print the PDF?

Yes. The pictures can reach the edges of the page, and a printer that cannot print to the edge may trim a thin strip. Use your printer's “fit to page” setting if that matters.

### Can I add HEIC, WebP or TIFF pictures?

Not yet. This page takes JPG and PNG only, and turns away other files by name. An iPhone usually turns HEIC photos into JPG when you choose them here. For other kinds, open the picture and save it as a JPG or PNG first.

### Does the PDF keep the pictures' details?

No. The camera, the date, the place a photo was taken and any notes stored in the pictures are not copied into the PDF. The PDF has no title or author either.

### Can I change the order after converting?

Press Start again and choose the pictures again, or reorder the pages of the PDF with Reorder pages.
