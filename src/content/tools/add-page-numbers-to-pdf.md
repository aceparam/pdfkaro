## How to number the pages

1. Choose the PDF file. You can also drop it anywhere on this page, or paste it.
2. Under “Where the numbers go”, choose a place. Bottom centre is chosen at first.
3. Press “Add numbers and download”.
4. When the new file is ready, press Download.

## Questions

### Is my file uploaded?

No. The file is read and the numbers are added in your browser, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Once you choose a file, the page loads only the parts of this site it needs to do the work, and they carry nothing from your file. When you press Start again or leave the page, the page forgets the file and the result.

### Where can the numbers go, and how large are they?

At the bottom or the top of each page, on the left, in the centre or on the right. A number is the digits alone, such as 12, in black. On an A4 or Letter page it is about the size of small print, a little over 1 cm from the edge. It is larger on a large page and smaller on a small one. Each number sits upright as the page is shown, even on a page that is turned or a different size.

### Can I leave out the cover?

Not in this tool. Every page is numbered, starting from 1 on the first page. To leave the cover unnumbered, take it off first with Split PDF or Extract pages, number the rest here, then put the two back together with Merge PDF. The first page after the cover will then show 1.

### What stays the same?

Everything already on the pages and in the file: text, links, form fields and what was typed in them, notes, bookmarks, attached files, the document's details and accessibility tags. The number is marked as a page number, so a screen reader skips it.

### What does not stay the same?

The new file is written afresh, so earlier saved versions stored inside the original are not kept, and a digital signature will no longer be valid. A file made to meet PDF/A, for long-term keeping, no longer does.

### Why can't I see a number on one page?

Form fields and notes are drawn above the page. If one sits where the number goes, it hides the number. Choose another place and number the file again.

### Can I use a PDF that has a password?

Yes, if you know the password. The page asks for it, and the password stays in your browser. The new file has no password and no restrictions. You can add a password afterwards.

### Is my original file changed?

No. You get a new file and your original stays as it was. Numbering report.pdf gives report-numbered.pdf.
