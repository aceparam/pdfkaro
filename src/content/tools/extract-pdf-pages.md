## How to extract pages from a PDF

1. Choose the PDF file you want pages from. You can also drop it anywhere on this page, or paste it.
2. Each page is shown. Press “Take” under each page you want. Press it again to leave the page out.
3. Press Extract and download.
4. When the new file is ready, press Download.

## Questions

### Is my file uploaded?

No. The file is read and the new file is made in your browser, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Once you choose a file, the page loads only the parts of this site it needs to do the work, and they carry nothing from your file. When you press Start again or leave the page, the page forgets the file, your choices and the result.

### Is my original file changed?

No. The pages you take are copied into a new file. Your original file stays as it was.

### What do I get?

One PDF with the pages you chose, in their original order. If you take pages 7, 2 and 9, the new file has pages 2, 7 and 9, in that order. Taking pages from report.pdf gives report-extracted.pdf.

### What happens to bookmarks and attached files?

The new file keeps the bookmarks that point to the pages you took, and drops the others. Files attached to the document are kept. Text, links and form fields on the pages you took stay as they were.

### Does anything from the other pages come over?

The pages you did not take are left out, with their text, and usually their form fields and notes. But some parts of a PDF belong to the whole document, and come over whole: its title, author and other details, its accessibility tags, the names of links inside it, and reading threads. These can still describe or name pages you did not take. In a file with accessibility tags, the tags can also bring along those pages' form fields, with what was typed in them, and their notes. If that matters, check the new file before you share it.

### Can I use a PDF that has a password?

Yes, if you know the password. The page asks for it, and the password stays in your browser. The new file has no password and no restrictions. You can add a password afterwards.

### Is there a limit on size?

There is no fixed limit. The work happens on your device, so a very large file can be slow, and a phone may run out of memory. The page warns you when the file is more than 100 MB, or has more than 20,000 pages.

### What if the file is damaged?

The page says it cannot read the file. You can choose another file.
