## How to turn PDF pages into PNG pictures

1. Choose the PDF file. You can also drop it anywhere on this page, or paste it.
2. Press “Convert to PNG and download”. There is nothing to set.
3. When the pictures are ready, press Download.

## Questions

### Does my file leave my device?

No. Your browser draws the pages itself, and nothing is sent anywhere. When you press Start again or leave the page, it forgets the file and the pictures.

### Why choose PNG rather than JPG?

PNG keeps every pixel exactly as it was drawn, without loss. Text, tables, charts and line drawings keep clean edges, with none of the blur a JPG can leave around small letters. For a page you want to read closely or show to others, PNG is the better choice.

### Can I put a page into a slide or a report?

Yes. Insert the PNG as a picture in PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides or Word, and crop it to the part you need. Each page is drawn at 150 pixels to the inch, so an A4 page is 1240 by 1754 pixels and stays clear on a projector or a laptop screen.

### Why are the PNG files bigger than my PDF?

A PDF stores text as letters and shapes, which take little room. A picture stores every pixel, and PNG packs them without throwing any detail away, so a page of text can take several hundred kilobytes. If the files are too large to send, PDF to JPG makes smaller ones, a little less sharp.

### What do I download?

A one-page PDF gives a single picture, such as report.png. A longer PDF gives one zip, report.zip, holding report-page-01.png, report-page-02.png and so on, in page order.

### How do I get the pictures out of the zip on a phone?

On an iPhone, tap the zip in the Files app and the pictures appear in a folder next to it. On Android, open it in your file app and choose Extract.

### Can I convert only some pages?

Every page becomes a picture. For only some pages, use Extract pages first to make a PDF of those pages, then convert it here.

### What happens to a poster or a large plan?

Very large pages are drawn smaller, keeping their shape, so the picture is not too big for a browser or a phone to open. They are never cut into pieces.

### Can I search or select the text in a PNG?

No. The picture is a drawing of the page, so its text cannot be searched or copied and its links do nothing. Your PDF stays as it was.

### Why are some words missing?

In some PDFs, Chinese, Japanese or Korean text may not appear in the pictures, even when the file includes its fonts. This tool leaves out the extra data needed to draw such text.
