## How to turn PDF pages into JPG pictures

1. Choose the PDF file. You can also drop it anywhere on this page, or paste it.
2. Press “Convert to JPG and download”. There are no settings to choose.
3. When the pictures are ready, press Download.

## Questions

### Is my file uploaded?

No. The pages are drawn in your browser, on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere. When you press Start again or leave the page, the page forgets the file and the pictures.

### Why turn a page into a JPG?

Almost every phone, chat app and website takes a JPG. It is the simplest way to send one page of a PDF as a photo, or to upload it to a form that only accepts pictures.

### What do I get?

One JPG for each page, in page order. A PDF of one page gives one picture named after the file, such as report.jpg. A longer PDF gives one zip file, report.zip. A 12-page PDF gives report-page-01.jpg to report-page-12.jpg: the numbers are padded to the same length, so the files sort in page order.

### How do I open the zip on my phone?

On an iPhone, tap the zip in the Files app, and the pictures appear in a folder beside it. On Android, open it in your file app and choose Extract.

### I need only one page. Can I get just that?

Every page becomes a picture. For one page, or a few, first make a PDF of only those pages with Extract pages, then convert that.

### How large are the pictures?

Each page is drawn at 150 pixels to the inch: an A4 page becomes 1240 by 1754 pixels, often a few hundred kilobytes. That is sharp on a phone or laptop screen. Very large pages, such as posters and plans, are drawn smaller, so that a phone or a browser can open them.

### Why does the text look a little soft?

JPG is made for photos. It keeps files small by smoothing fine detail, so small letters can look slightly fuzzy up close. For pages of text or line drawings, PDF to PNG keeps the edges sharp, in larger files.

### Can I copy the text or click the links?

No. A picture is a drawing of the page: its text cannot be searched or copied, and its links cannot be clicked. Your PDF itself is not changed, so keep it for that.

### Some text is missing from the pictures. Why?

In some PDFs, Chinese, Japanese or Korean text may be missing, whether or not the file includes its fonts. This tool does not carry the extra data needed to draw such text.

### Is anything else put into the pictures?

No. Each picture holds only the drawn page, with no title, author or date from the file. Form fields are drawn with what was typed in them, and notes and stamps as the page shows them.
