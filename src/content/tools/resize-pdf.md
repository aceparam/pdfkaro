## How to resize the pages of a PDF

1. Choose the PDF file. You can also drop it anywhere on this page, or paste it.
2. Choose A4 or Letter.
3. Press “Resize and download”, and when the new file is ready, press Download.

## Questions

### Is my file uploaded?

No. The new file is made in your browser, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the parts of this site the page loads carry nothing from your file.

### What happens to a page of another shape?

It is made smaller or larger to fit the paper, keeping its shape, and centred. So it gets white space at two edges. Nothing is cut off.

### Will a small page be made larger?

Yes, to fill the paper. A small scan made larger may print less sharp.

### Which way round will my pages be?

Each page stays the way round it was. A page on its side becomes A4 or Letter on its side. A square page becomes upright.

### Will parts I cropped out show again?

A part hidden by cropping stays hidden, and it stays in the file. But a page with broken drawing instructions, whether made by mistake or on purpose, can show it again in some programs after resizing. Cropping or resizing is not a way to hide something private.

### Why did I get no new file?

Every page was already the paper size you chose. You can use your file as it is, or press Start again, choose the file again and pick the other size.

### Do links, forms and bookmarks still work?

Yes. Links, form fields and notes move with the page, and bookmarks still go to the same place; a note, link or form field that was wholly out of view is hidden and stays in the file, and one partly out of view may now show more of it, or all of it. Text you type into a form field afterwards may look larger on a page that was made much smaller, and a digital signature will no longer be valid.

### What is removed?

On each resized page: its bleed, trim and art boxes, which print shops use; its stored thumbnail; and measuring scales that some drawing programs keep.

### Will this make my file smaller?

No. Compress PDF does that.

### Can I use a PDF that has a password?

Yes, if you know the password. The page asks for it, and the password stays in your browser. The new file has no password and no restrictions.

### Is my original file changed?

No. You get a new file. Resizing report.pdf gives report-resized.pdf.
