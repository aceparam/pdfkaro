Merge PDF files

Combine several PDFs into one. Files stay on your device.

You can also drop files anywhere on this page, or paste them.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Choose the PDF files you want to combine.
  2. Put them in the order you want. Drag them, or use the Move up and Move down buttons.
  3. Press Merge and download.
  4. When the new file is ready, press Download.

Questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The files are read and merged in your browser. They are not sent anywhere.

Is there a limit on size or number of files?

There is no fixed limit. The work happens on your device, so very large files can be slow, and a phone may run out of memory. The page warns you when the files add up to more than 100 MB.

Can I merge a PDF that has a password?

Yes, if you know the password. The page asks for it when it reads the file. The password stays in your browser.
